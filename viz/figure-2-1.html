Why invest in agricultural water management?

It delivers against three strategic objectives at once - people, prosperity, and planet.

People

Jobs

-20% childhood stunting via water access

+245 million jobs (direct and indirect globally), of which about 218 million are in Sub-Saharan Africa

Prosperity

Higher productivity

Up to $4 in economic returns for every $1 invested

A 10% gain in agricultural productivity can reduce the likelihood of poverty by 2.5% to 3%

Planet

Climate resilience

Higher yields mean less land pressure

Irrigation boosts soil carbon by 6%

Alternate wetting and drying reduce methane emissions by up to 53%
