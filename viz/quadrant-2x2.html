Water-Food Compass: Where does your country stand?

Every country sits in one of four contexts, set by its freshwater stress and whether it is a net food importer or exporter.

Hover a point for its profile, or search and click a quadrant label to zoom in.

Source: WRI Aqueduct 4.0 (baseline water stress) and FAOSTAT food balance sheets (2019). Context labels per Figure 3.1 of the published Nourish and Flourish report. Thresholds: water stress ≥ 40% (high stress), net food calorie imports > 0 (importer). Borderline countries may land in a different context here than in the report's narrative (e.g. Peru sits within ~0.01% of the importer/exporter line).

About this data

A country's food trade position is measured by whether it is a net importer or exporter of calories, based on FAO food balance sheets. This focuses on food crops - data limitations mean fiber and non-food export crops such as coffee and cocoa are not included. A country's position reflects a national average; most countries contain diverse subnational contexts that may differ significantly from the overall picture.
